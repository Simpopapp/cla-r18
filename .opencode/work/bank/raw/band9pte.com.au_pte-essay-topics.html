How to Score High on PTE Essay Writing

PTE Academic scores the Write Essay task across four criteria: Content (relevance and development of ideas), Form (word count between 200–300), Grammar (accuracy and sentence variety), and Vocabulary (range and precision of word choice). Each criterion is worth up to 2 marks, for a maximum total of 2 points per task — but Writing also contributes to the Enabling Skills sections, affecting your overall Writing score significantly.

PTE Essay Types Explained

✅ Agree / Disagree
State a clear position in the introduction and defend it with two separate arguments and evidence. Avoid switching sides.
⚖️ Discuss Both Views
Dedicate one body paragraph to each view with equal depth. Give your own opinion — usually in the conclusion.
🔧 Problem / Solution
Identify specific causes in one body paragraph, then propose matching solutions in the next. Pair each cause with a concrete remedy.

Common PTE Essay Mistakes to Avoid

  • Vague examples: Instead of "some studies show," name the institution and year.
  • Opinion in Discuss essays: Save your personal view for the conclusion, not the introduction.
  • Covering too many points: Two well-developed arguments outperform four underdeveloped ones.
  • Exceeding 300 words: PTE does not reward length — quality and accuracy matter more.
  • Informal vocabulary: Replace "kids" with "children," "good" with "beneficial," "bad" with "detrimental."
  • Repetition: Use synonyms and nominalisations to demonstrate vocabulary range.
