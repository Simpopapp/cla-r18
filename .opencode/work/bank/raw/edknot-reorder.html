Frequently asked questions

How is Reorder Paragraph scored?

One point for each adjacent pair in the correct order, so a text of five paragraphs is worth four points. Getting the first two right earns a point even if the rest is wrong.

How do I find the first paragraph?

It introduces the topic with a full noun phrase and no pronoun or linking word pointing back to an earlier sentence.

What links the rest?

Pronouns, repeated nouns, and connectives such as however, this and these. Each paragraph refers back to the one before it.
